Core rules:
- Never signal in the middle of a range.
- Adjacent similar wicks count as one test, not several independent tests.
- A first reversal after a climax is observation only. A/B requires failed continuation, reclaim, opposite follow-through, or a second reversal already visible by this close.
- A wide-channel trend-following setup is downgraded one grade.
- A rolling high/low is not by itself a range or channel boundary.
- An EMA touch is supporting evidence only; it cannot define the market state or boundary.

High-recall lifecycle rules:
- `candidate_lifecycle=watch` is normally observation. Promote it only if the current closed bar itself already contains independent reclaim/follow-through evidence.
- `candidate_lifecycle=confirmed` means the deterministic gate saw a later confirmation, but you must verify the source observation and the confirmation from `recent_ohlc`; do not trust the label.
- Inspect every item in `hypotheses`. Multiple hypotheses may coexist until the chart evidence selects one.
- A displacement candidate must break a genuine pre-existing boundary and close away from it. Breaking only an arbitrary 8/12/24-bar extreme is insufficient.
- A sweep candidate must touch or exceed a defensible boundary and close back appropriately. If the boundary was not genuinely established, downgrade or reject it.
- A pullback candidate must first prove a narrow channel or a directional leg and must pull back to a favorable location; an EMA touch alone is insufficient.
- A double top/bottom or wedge requires separated structural pushes. Adjacent wicks are one push.
- If the current setup still requires a future bar, it is not A/B.

Wide-channel requirements:

- Independently verify the validator from `recent_ohlc`; never override a failed gate by manually choosing convenient minor highs/lows.
- A wide channel needs the latest structure—not a stale or cherry-picked subsection—to contain at least three upper and three lower structural anchors in an alternating sequence.
- Consecutive anchors on the same side must be at least four 5-minute bars apart. Equal/adjacent wick clusters are one anchor.
- Both boundaries must trend in the same direction and be approximately parallel; their absolute slope ratio must remain between 0.5 and 2.0 and projected width must remain reasonably stable.
- At least two countertrend rotations must each last at least three bars, contain at least two countertrend close advances, and progress at least 0.60 ATR. A one/two-bar wick bounce is not a wide-channel swing.
- Projected channel width must be at least 1.50 ATR. A falling/rising zigzag, rolling highs/lows, or a single directional leg with small pauses is not a wide channel.
- Every accepted anchor must include Beijing time and price, channel slope/direction, the tested edge, and the validator metrics in `context_json`.
